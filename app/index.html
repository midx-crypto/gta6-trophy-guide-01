    Roadmap to the platinum: Vice Got a Price!

    One playthrough, five chapters, about 60 to 80 hours of story and about 200 hours to the platinum (PowerPyx). No mission replay and no mission medals, so play at your own pace; free roam continues after the story. Keep cheats off: they disable trophies and saving. Save at the start and end of each chapter: 15 manual slots plus 1 autosave.

      After the finale, the 100% checklist (Progress, then Stats, then 100% Completion) is the last big job. PowerPyx calls it lenient, about 60% of true completion: 70 of 80 challenges, 15 of 18 meditations, 300 stunts, all robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret’s quests, and 15 of 18 card sets. Then A Long Road pops, and the platinum follows.

      Missable watch

      Checked safe: looks missable, is not

      • Kenton goes quiet after Chapter 1 and returns later. His treasure diving strand, and its trophy, are not missable. (PowerPyx)
      • The Kingpin Bowling robbery comes back later. Let’s Go Bowling is not missable. (PowerPyx)
      • Optional missions disappear over time, but there are far more than the 100 the checklist needs. (PowerPyx)
      • Some fences leave for good. None of them has a trophy quest. (PowerPyx)
      • A few magazine collectibles sit in story buildings that close off. They also spawn elsewhere. (PowerPyx)
      • Quests with an hourglass icon can fail on a timer. None is tied to a trophy. (PowerPyx)
      • All 12 story trophies are unmissable. They pop automatically as the story plays. (PowerPyx, Sixshore)
